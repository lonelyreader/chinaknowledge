# Product

## Register

product

## Users

China, in Fact serves people outside China who want to understand the country through specific people, their current work, questions, experiences, and published knowledge. It also serves participating Chinese members who need a durable public page for their projects, work, interests, and chosen connection paths.

The primary visitor task is to discover a person through something concrete, understand why that person is interesting, and continue through their profile, public links, Discord, or related content. The primary member task is to remain accurately and respectfully discoverable without being reduced to a uniform role.

## Product Purpose

China, in Fact is a public people network that helps the world meet real Chinese people through what they are doing, making, asking, and publishing. People are the central object; Stories, Guides, Places, and projects provide evidence and routes into those people.

Success means visitors enter Person pages and continue to relevant projects, public links, or Discord, while members are willing to keep their public presence current. The site organizes and verifies information without presenting itself as a magazine, talent marketplace, staff directory, or social ranking system.

## Brand Personality

Specific, warm, and clear. The product should feel inhabited by real people, comfortable enough for curiosity, and disciplined enough to earn trust. It is contemporary and international without flattening people into generic creator profiles or relying on cultural decoration.

## Anti-references

- Editorial portrait magazines, personality features, and large profile spotlights.
- Corporate team grids, creator directories, talent marketplaces, rankings, follower metrics, or availability badges.
- Generic social feeds, fake activity, chat mirrors, and engagement counters.
- Red-and-gold China motifs, decorative calligraphy, lanterns, dragons, ink-wash effects, flag palettes, or cold technology-blue branding.
- Generic AI landing pages built from oversized pills, glass cards, gradients, repeated tracked eyebrows, or explanatory product copy.

## Design Principles

1. Start with a person and a current action. Content and places should lead back to specific people.
2. Make continuation obvious. A profile, project, public link, or Discord path should be a natural next step.
3. Keep people complex. Show only real public evidence and never invent status, popularity, expertise, or uniform identities.
4. Use structure to explain. Hierarchy, layout, state, and affordance must do the work that helper prose would otherwise attempt.
5. Share one system across connection, discovery, reading, and utility pages while preserving each page type's actual job.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA for text, controls, focus, and state communication. Touch targets are at least 44px on mobile. Status is never communicated by color alone. Public English and Spanish remain independently readable, long labels may wrap, reduced motion is respected, and essential content is never hidden behind hover or animation.
